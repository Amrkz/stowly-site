How to compress a PDF

  • Open Stowly and drag one or more PDFs into the window, or add them with the file picker.
  • Choose a profile: Basic, Balanced, or Strong.
  • Choose how to save: a copy beside the original, a copy in a folder you pick, or replace the original and keep a backup.
  • Start compression. The queue shows the original size, the new size, and your savings.

Stowly never writes an output file larger than the original. Text stays selectable on typical documents, and links, bookmarks, and metadata are kept.

Profiles

  • Basic — a lighter touch. Images are downsampled to about 150 dpi.
  • Balanced — the everyday choice, about 110 dpi. Use this for most files.
  • Strong — maximum shrinkage, about 72 dpi, when a smaller file matters most.

Results vary by document. Image-heavy and scanned PDFs usually shrink more than text-only files.

Free use and Stowly Unlimited

You can compress five files per day at no charge. The counter is stored only on your Mac and resets each calendar day.

Stowly Unlimited is a one-time in-app purchase that removes the daily limit. There is no subscription. Buy or restore it from the unlock sheet in the app. Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store.

Password-protected PDFs

Stowly refuses password-protected PDFs and shows a clear message. It does not ask for, store, or attempt to bypass the password. Remove the password in another app if you want to compress a copy of that file.

Files and privacy

Compression happens only on your Mac. Stowly does not upload files, does not create an account, and does not collect personal data. For the full policy, see the Privacy Policy.
