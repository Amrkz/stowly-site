Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Stowly is an offline PDF compressor for macOS, published by Dr Amr Qazzaz. This policy describes what the app does — and does not do — with your information.

Data not collected

Stowly does not collect, store on a server, sell, share, or otherwise process personal data. There is no developer backend, no user account, no mailing list, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party crash or telemetry service.

In App Store terms: Data Not Collected.

Your files stay on your Mac

PDFs you open in Stowly are read and compressed on your computer. Files are not uploaded and do not leave your Mac. Stowly does not require network access to compress documents.

You choose where the result is saved: a copy beside the original, a copy in a folder you pick, or a replacement of the original with a local backup. Stowly does not keep a library or a cloud copy of your documents.

What is stored locally

Stowly may keep a small amount of data on your Mac so the app can work as designed, for example:

  • Your settings, such as the selected compression profile and save mode.
  • A local counter of how many files you have compressed today, used only to apply the free daily limit of five files.
  • A local record of whether Stowly Unlimited has been unlocked on this Mac, after a purchase or a restore through the App Store.

This information stays on your device. It is not transmitted to the developer.

Retention and deletion

That information is kept only on your Mac, and only for as long as you keep it there. Stowly does not store a copy on a server, so the developer has nothing to delete remotely and there is no account to close. Because the app does not collect personal data, there is no consent to withdraw. You can remove the settings, the daily counter, and the local unlock record by uninstalling Stowly or by deleting the data Stowly saved on your Mac. Questions about your information can be sent to amroqazzaz@gmail.com.

In-app purchases

Stowly offers an optional one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase called Stowly Unlimited, which removes the five-file daily limit. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Stowly does not receive your payment card details, Apple ID password, or billing address. The developer does not operate a payment server.

Network use

Stowly does not include its own networking features, accounts, or analytics. Compressing a PDF does not contact a remote server. If you buy or restore Stowly Unlimited, Apple's App Store and StoreKit services handle that transaction. That communication is between your device and Apple, under Apple's policies.

Children

Stowly does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Stowly will not begin collecting personal data without an updated policy and, where required, an updated App Store privacy label.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about Stowly can be sent to amroqazzaz@gmail.com.
